## Keywords

### App Store Keywords
**Maximum: 100 characters** (comma-separated, no spaces after commas)

```
calorie tracker,macro,nutrition,fitness,diet,food log,strava,bmr,tdee,weight loss,muscle gain
```

**Keyword Strategy**:
- ✅ calorie tracker (high volume)
- ✅ macro (common abbreviation)
- ✅ nutrition (broad category)
- ✅ fitness (broad audience)
- ✅ diet (high search volume)
- ✅ food log (specific feature)
- ✅ strava (unique differentiator)
- ✅ bmr, tdee (specific features)
- ✅ weight loss, muscle gain (user goals)

**Avoid**:
- ❌ Don't repeat words from app name/subtitle
- ❌ Don't use spaces (wastes characters)
- ❌ Don't use plural forms (Apple handles this)
- ❌ Don't use competitor names

## Screenshots

### Required Sizes

**iPhone:**
- 6.7" Display (1290 x 2796 pixels) - iPhone 15 Pro Max, 14 Pro Max, etc.
- 6.5" Display (1242 x 2688 pixels) - iPhone 11 Pro Max, XS Max, etc.

**iPad:**
- 12.9" Display (2048 x 2732 pixels) - iPad Pro 12.9"

**Minimum**: 3-10 screenshots per device type

### Screenshot Order (Recommended)

1. **Home Screen** - Show daily summary, calorie balance
2. **Food Logging** - Demonstrate easy meal entry
3. **Strava Integration** - Show activity sync
4. **Calendar View** - Display nutrition history
5. **Analytics** - Charts and progress tracking
6. **Goals Setup** - BMR/TDEE calculator
7. **Progress Photos** - Transformation timeline

### Screenshot Design Guidelines

**What to Include:**
- ✅ Real (or realistic) data
- ✅ Annotations/callouts for key features
- ✅ Consistent status bar (9:41, full battery, signal)
- ✅ Your app's color scheme
- ✅ Captions explaining each feature

**What to Avoid:**
- ❌ Empty screens or placeholder data
- ❌ Screenshots with errors
- ❌ Inconsistent UI between shots
- ❌ Text too small to read
- ❌ Adult content, violence, etc.
